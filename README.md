## 📌 About Me

I am an **Independent Systems Architect** and **Certified Senior Systems Analyst** specializing in autonomous software engineering, deterministic architecture governance, and human-AI cognitive collaboration.

Over the past decade, my practice has centered on mission-critical system architecture, enterprise platforms, and regulatory compliance. Throughout 2026, operating as a **solo architect steering autonomous multi-agent workflows**, I engineered and delivered an enterprise-grade Laboratory Information Management System (**AMF-LIMS**) spanning **435K+ canonical `cloc` SLOC (>526K portfolio SLOC incl. documentation & specs)** across 10 mission-critical domains under strict **FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and GAMP 5** data integrity standards.

My research and engineering practice focus on:
1. **Modern Regulatory LIMS & Applied AI**: Architecting next-generation laboratory platforms with integrated agentic execution, electronic lab notebooks (ELN), and automated GxP audit trails.
2. **Autonomous Software Engineering & Telemetry (SE)**: Mitigating context decay, structural drift, and deceptive technical debt across **100B+ cumulative context tokens (surpassed 130B+ in continuous live production)**.
3. **Cognitive Prosthetics & Neurodiversity (HCI)**: Exploring high-agency human-AI coupling and external prefrontal scaffolding for twice-exceptional (2E) architects.
